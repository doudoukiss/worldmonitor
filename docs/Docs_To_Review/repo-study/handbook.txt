Yes. If you close a panel like TV or webcams, the reliable way to bring it back is through the panel toggles in the settings UI. The close
  button only sets that panel’s enabled flag to false in saved panel settings, and the settings modal can turn it back on again in src/app/event-
  handlers.ts:340 and src/components/UnifiedSettings.ts:333. There is also a quick undo for the most recently closed panel with Cmd+Z / Ctrl+Z in
  src/app/event-handlers.ts:128 and src/app/event-handlers.ts:400.

  For TV and webcams specifically: they are among the heavier local consumers, because the actual video playback and decode happen in your
  browser on your Mac. The good news is that closing those panels really does stop the video side. The webcam panel destroys its iframes when
  hidden or closed in src/components/LiveWebcamsPanel.ts:578 and src/components/LiveWebcamsPanel.ts:684. The TV panel destroys the player and
  pauses native video in src/components/LiveNewsPanel.ts:655 and src/components/LiveNewsPanel.ts:1616. So if you do not need video, disabling
  those panels is a sensible bandwidth and CPU reduction. There is also a built-in media setting for lower stream quality and for turning off
  “keep live streams running” in src/services/preferences-content.ts:205 and src/services/live-stream-settings.ts:23. That said, closing the
  panels does not stop unrelated news/API/background polling elsewhere in the app; it stops the video playback path.
